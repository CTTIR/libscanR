# User-supplied atomic references

No NIST Atomic Spectra Database compilation, selected source-row extract or
historical atomic table is included in this source tree or installed package.
The software does not download a reference automatically.

Users may obtain their own data from https://physics.nist.gov/asd and convert a
local CSV using the documented helper. They are responsible for the applicable
provider terms and citation. The MIT software license does not grant rights in
user-supplied reference data. In particular, NIST ASD is Standard Reference
Database 78; NIST's general statement for non-SRD material must not be assumed
to authorize redistribution of SRD compilations.

Provider information:

- https://www.nist.gov/pml/atomic-spectra-database
- https://www.nist.gov/open/copyright-fair-use-and-licensing-statements-srd-data-software-and-technical-series-publications
- https://doi.org/10.18434/T4W30F

The bundled teaching peaks and Saha analytical fixture are fictional software
examples, not copied atomic data or experimental observations. NIST has not
evaluated or endorsed these algorithms, examples or experimental conclusions.
